Intercept Echo turns an eight-bit register into a fast, tactile puzzle. Shift, rotate, and transform visible bit patterns to match incoming targets before the clock runs out — no prior knowledge of binary or programming required.

CRACK THE TRANSMISSION
Work through the Intercept campaign, cracking intercepted messages letter by letter. Each letter is a register puzzle; solve enough of them and the message reveals itself.

SHIFT AND ROTATE
Learn the register one mechanic at a time: Count (tap cells to set a value), Shift (double and halve, with bits spilling off the ends as a resource), and Rotate (wrap a bit around instead of losing it). Every operation is exact and deterministic — no luck involved.

SCORED, NOT JUST TIMED
Solve a puzzle in the fewest moves for the best score. Extra moves cost points, so efficient play is rewarded, not just speed.
